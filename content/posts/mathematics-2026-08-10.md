## AI Makes Waves in Mathematics: Jacobian Conjecture Disproved with Machine Assistance

**August 10, 2026** – The world of mathematics is abuzz with the recent announcement that a long-standing problem, the Jacobian conjecture, has been partially disproved with the crucial assistance of artificial intelligence. Just days ago, on August 5, 2026, a mathematician utilizing Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 AI model reportedly uncovered a remarkably simple counterexample, demonstrating the conjecture to be false in three dimensions and above.

This breakthrough highlights the rapidly evolving role of AI as a powerful tool in advanced mathematical research. For over 80 years, the Jacobian conjecture, an abstract problem in algebraic geometry, has resisted attempts at proof or disproof by human mathematicians. Its disproof, even in higher dimensions, marks a significant moment, showing that AI can do more than just solve known problems; it can help uncover elusive patterns and objects that lead to new fundamental insights.

The development follows a growing trend where AI systems are actively contributing to mathematical discovery, moving beyond competitive puzzles to genuinely assisting in research-level problems. As these intelligent tools become more sophisticated, they are opening unprecedented avenues for exploration and accelerating the pace of mathematical understanding.
