## AI Redefines Mathematical Frontiers: A New Era of Discovery and Verification

As of November 2026, the landscape of mathematics is undergoing a profound transformation, driven significantly by advancements in Artificial Intelligence. The year has seen AI systems not only assist but actively contribute to solving long-standing mathematical problems, marking a new chapter in how mathematical research is conducted and verified.

One of the most notable developments occurred in 2026 when an AI system, LeanMind, in collaboration with researchers, discovered a proof for a specific case of the Sylvester-Gallai conjecture. This breakthrough was subsequently formally verified using the Coq proof assistant, representing a historic instance of AI solving a major open problem with minimal human guidance. This achievement highlights AI's growing capacity to generate novel mathematical ideas and proofs.

Further pushing the boundaries, OpenAI announced in September 2026 that an internal AI model had made significant progress on the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems, although the solution is still awaiting full verification by the broader mathematical community. Additionally, AI has tackled and resolved several of Paul Erdős's conjectures, overturning long-standing predictions in May 2026 and autonomously resolving Erdős Problem #728 in January 2026.

This surge in AI-driven discovery is deeply intertwined with the maturation of formal proof assistants like Lean, Coq, and Isabelle. These tools provide rigorous frameworks for expressing and verifying mathematical statements, and their growing libraries of formalized mathematics serve as rich training data for AI. Some leading mathematics journals are now even requiring formalization for proofs that rely on extensive computation, underscoring the shift towards machine-checkable rigor.

The increasing integration of AI is prompting mathematicians to re-evaluate their roles, shifting focus from merely problem-solving to posing intriguing questions and exploring new conceptual avenues. This evolving dynamic promises a future where human intuition and computational power combine to unlock previously intractable mathematical mysteries.
