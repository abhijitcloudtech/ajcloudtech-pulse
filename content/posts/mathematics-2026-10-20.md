**Mathematics in a New Era: AI Takes Center Stage in 2026**

As of October 20, 2026, the landscape of mathematical research is undergoing a profound transformation, driven largely by the accelerating capabilities of artificial intelligence. What was once the exclusive domain of human intuition and rigorous manual proof is now a collaborative frontier, with AI models demonstrating an unprecedented ability to tackle longstanding open problems.

The year 2025 marked a pivotal moment, with AI systems moving beyond competitive puzzles to genuinely contribute to advanced mathematical research. This trend has only intensified in 2026. Just last week, OpenAI announced solutions to 719 previously unsolved mathematical problems, showcasing the remarkable power of their latest models. Similarly, Google DeepMind's systems have produced verified proofs for nine open problems from Paul Erdős's famous list, along with 44 other open conjectures, a significant stride in automated discovery. Even specific, complex challenges like the 31-year-old Ghys conjecture have seen breakthroughs thanks to human-AI collaboration, culminating in machine-verified Lean proofs.

This rapid progress has elicited strong reactions within the mathematical community. Fields Medal winner Hugo Duminil-Copin acknowledged the dramatic shift, stating, "All the avenues of research I had are now gone; we're facing a new day". While the emotional impact is palpable, figures like Stéphane Mallat, a 2025 Gold Medal recipient, emphasize that the essence of mathematics—clear human thinking—remains crucial, even as the tools evolve. Recognizing this shift, the Simons Foundation, in collaboration with OpenAI and Anthropic, has launched a $15 million program to help mathematicians adapt, fostering workshops and establishing standards for AI's integration into research. This ensures that as AI becomes an indispensable partner, the integrity and rigor of mathematical discovery are maintained.

This era heralds not the end of mathematical exploration, but a new "golden age" where human ingenuity, amplified by powerful AI, promises to unlock deeper insights into the universe's fundamental structures.
